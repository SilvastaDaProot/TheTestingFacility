About the Facility

I'm passionate about creating and experimenting with technology. My current focus is on building a robust self-hosted environment — Proxmox on two servers with a Raspberry Pi quorum device, running multiple virtual machines for various purposes.

Everything here is a testbed: services get broken, fixed, and occasionally improved. That's the point.
